# Follow-up email shape

Subject: <Company> x <Your company>: next steps from <day>

Hi <first name>,

Thanks for the time <today / on day>. <One line on what mattered to them, in their words.>

As discussed:
- <what you will do> by <date stated in the meeting>
- <what they will do>

<One clear question or proposed next step with a date.>

<Sign-off>

Checks before showing the draft:
- Every promise appears in the transcript or notes.
- Dates were stated in the meeting, or are clearly offered as proposals.
- Recipient name and address verified against a live source.
- Australian English. No em dashes. Under 150 words.
