# CRM note shape

<Date> - <meeting type> with <names and roles>

What happened: <2 lines>
What they care about: <1-2 points, with who said it>
Decision process: <who decides, by when, what they need>
Next step: <owner> to <action> by <date>
Risks: <1 line>
Source: <transcript or thread reference>

Field changes go in a separate table: field, current value, proposed value, evidence.
